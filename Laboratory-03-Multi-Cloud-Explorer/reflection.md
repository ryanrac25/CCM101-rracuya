# Mission Reflection

This mission helped me understand that choosing a cloud platform is not simply about selecting the most popular provider. Among AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform, Google Cloud impressed me the most because of its strong capabilities in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. I found these technologies interesting because they support modern applications and cloud-native development.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide essential cloud services such as virtual machines, object storage, networking, identity and access management, databases, and container technologies. They also provide scalable infrastructure that allows organizations to increase or decrease resources depending on their workload requirements.

Business requirements have a major influence on cloud platform selection. For example, a university that already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory may benefit more from Azure because of its integration with Microsoft technologies. An AI research company may prefer Google Cloud because of its AI and machine learning capabilities, while a startup or global application may choose AWS because of its broad range of services and scalability.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to analyze the client's actual needs before recommending a cloud provider. I learned that technical decisions should be connected to business requirements, budget, existing technologies, scalability, performance, and future growth.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has also improved significantly after completing this mission. I added organized research documents, comparison tables, client recommendations, decision matrices, screenshots, and Linux investigation results. The portfolio now provides clearer evidence of my cloud computing activities and shows my ability to document and evaluate different cloud solutions.
